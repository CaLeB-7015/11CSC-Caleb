Sea Level Rise

About

Sea level rise happens when the ocean becomes higher because of climate change. As the Earth gets warmer, glaciers and ice sheets melt, adding more water to the oceans. Warmer ocean water also expands, causing sea levels to rise even more.

Human activities such as burning fossil fuels release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere. These gases trap heat, leading to global warming and causing sea levels to continue rising.

Some of the effects of sea level rise include:

-Coastal flooding -Beach erosion -Damage to homes and roads -Loss of habitats for wildlife -Increased risks for people living near the coast

Learning about sea level rise helps people understand why climate change is important and what we can do to protect our environment for future generations.
